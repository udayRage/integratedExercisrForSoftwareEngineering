# integratedExercisrForSoftwareEngineering

Project for **IE04 Integrated Exercise for Software II** (University of Aizu, 2026).

## Lab Student Management System

A system that helps an instructor manage the students in their lab: registering students,
planning projects and milestones, scheduling meetings, and tracking progress. It is meant to
replace the spreadsheets currently used for this work.

The full requirements are in the RFP: [ie04_project2026_RFP.pdf](ie04_project2026_RFP.pdf).

### Required functions

- **Student registration** – instructors add and update students and sub-instructors.
- **Project planning** – instructors add projects and milestones and update their status.
- **Regular meeting scheduling** – the system suggests meeting slots that avoid students' classes
  and match instructor availability; the instructor makes the final choice.
- **Project status** – students, sub-instructors and the instructor can view meeting schedules,
  tasks and milestone status.
- **Calendar export** – meetings and milestones can be exported as iCalendar (`.ics`) files.
